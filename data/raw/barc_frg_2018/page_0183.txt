Method of application:

a) All of phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, zinc and two-third of nitrogen should be
applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) The remaining nitrogen should be applied at 30 days after transplanting.

c) Potassium should be applied in the form of K2SOx4 instead of muriate of potash
(MoP). The sulphur in K,SO, will be enough to meet up the sulphur requirement of
the crop and no additional sulphur will be necessary.
