2.4 Nutrient Uptake by Crops

Nutrient uptake by a crop is the resultant product of crop nutrient concentration and crop
yield including the crop residues. In general, higher the yield, higher is the removal of
nutrients. Modern crop varieties absorb relatively higher amount of nutrients than the
traditional varieties. Nutrient uptake by various crops is given in Table 1.
