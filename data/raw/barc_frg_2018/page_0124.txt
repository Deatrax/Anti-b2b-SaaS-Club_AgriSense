Method of application:

a) Half of organic manure and all of phosphorus, sulphur, zinc and boron should be
applied as basal during final land preparation. Remaining half organic manure should
be applied in pit before planting of seedlings.

b) Nitrogen and potassium should be applied in three equal splits at 10-15, 30 and 50
days after transplanting as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed
thoroughly with the soil as soon as possible for better utilization.

c) Instead of urea, Urea Supper Granules (USG) might be applied 7-8 cm deep into the
soil and 8—10 cm apart from the plant and as ring method.
