Method of application:

a) All fertilizers except nitrogen should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Nitrogen should be applied in two equal splits during the 2" and 4" weeks of
germination.

c) Rhizobium inoculum (at the rate of 50 g/kg seed) must be used if available and in that
case N fertilizer should not be used.
