BETEL LEAF (Piper betle)

(Var. BARI Pan-1, BARI Pan-2, BARI Pan-3)

Yield Goal: 35.0 +3.5 lac leaf/ha/year

Method of application:

a) One-third of oilcake and all of phosphorus, potassium and sulphur should be applied
as basal at the time of final land preparation.

b) After 30-40 days of planting, remaining oilcake should be applied at 15—20 days
interval in rows until the vine grows up to 1.0—1.5 meter in length.

c) Nitrogen should be applied in 3 equal installments before the monsoon rain and in
September and December.

d) In the subsequent years, the above fertilizer doses should be applied in 3 equal
installments, before the monsoon rains and in the month of September and December.
