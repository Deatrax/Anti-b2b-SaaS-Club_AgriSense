KENAF (Hibiscus cannabinus)

(Var. HC-2, HC-95 and BJRI Kenaf-3)

Yield Goal: 5.0 + 0.5 t/ha

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha)
Interpretation N P K
Optimum 0-30 0-6 0-17
Medium 31-60 7-12 18-34
Low 61-90 13-18 35-51
Very low 91-120 19-24 52-68
Method of application:

a) Half of nitrogen and all of phosphorus, potassium and organic manure (if used)
should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Remaining half nitrogen should be top dressed at 40-45 DAS.

c) Application of 3—5 t/ha organic manure (cowdung/poultry manure) is recommended.
In that case the dose of N, P, K and S should be reduced based on the rate of
application of organic manure as per Appendix-7.
