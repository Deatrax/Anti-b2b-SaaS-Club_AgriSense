9.1.2 FIBRE CROPS

JUTE (Corchorus capsularis)
[Var. CVL-1, CVE-3, CC-45, BJRI Deshi Pat-5 (BJC-7370), BJRI Deshi Pat-6 (BJC-

83), BJRI Deshi Pat-7 (BJC-2142, only for Faridpur region), Binadeshipat-2 and
Atompat -38 |

Yield Goal: 3.5 + 0.35 t/ha

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha)
Interpretation N P K S Zn
Optimum 0-25 0-4 0-13 0-5 -
Medium 26-50 5-8 14-26 6-10 0.0-1.5
Low 51-75 9-12 27-39 11-15 1.6-3.0
Very low 76-100 13-16 40-52 16-20 3.14.5
Method of application:

a) Half of nitrogen and all of phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, zinc and organic manure (if used)
should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Remaining nitrogen should be top dressed at 40-45 DAS under moist soil condition.

c) Application of 3—S t/ha organic manure (cowdung/poultry manure) is recommended. In that
case the dose of N, P, K and S should be reduced based on the rate of application of organic
manure as per Appendix-7.
