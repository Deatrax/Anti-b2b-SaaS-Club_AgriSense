Method of application:

a) All of organic manure, phosphorus, sulphur, zinc and boron should be applied as
basal during final land preparation.

b) Nitrogen and potassium should be applied in two equal splits at 15 and 35 days after
transplanting as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with
the soil as soon as possible for better utilization.

c) Instead of urea, Urea Supper Granules (USG) might be applied 7-8 cm deep into the
soil and 8—10 cm apart from the plant and as ring method.
