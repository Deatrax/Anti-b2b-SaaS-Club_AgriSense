Method of application:
a) All of organic manure, phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, zinc and boron should be
applied in pit 5—7 days before planting and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

b) Nitrogen should be applied around the plant as side dressing at 15, 35, 55 and 75 days
after planting under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil as soon
as possible for better utilization.
