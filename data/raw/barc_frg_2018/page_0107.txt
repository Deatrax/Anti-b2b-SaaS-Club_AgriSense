83) and BJRI Deshi Pat-7 (BJC-2142, only for Faridpur region)|

Yield Goal (Seed): 600-1000 kg/ha

Method of application:

a) All of phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, zinc, boron and organic manure (if used)
should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Nitrogen fertilizer should be applied as topdress in three equal splits, the first one-
third during final land preparation, the second one-third at 20-25 DAS and the
last one-third at 40-45 DAS.

c) Application of 3-5 t/ha organic manure (cowdung/poultry manure) is recommended.
In that case the dose of N, P, K and S should be reduced based on the rate of
application of organic manure as per Appendix-7.
