9.1.11 FODDER CROPS

NAPIER GRASS (Pennisetum purpureum)
(Var. IGFRI-3, IGFRI-6, IGFRI-7, IGFRI-10, CO-3)

Yield Goal (Green biomass): 370.0 + 37.0 t/ha/year

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha/year)
Interpretation N P K
Optimum 0-45 0-12 0-15
Medium 46-90 13-24 16-30
Low 91-135 25-36 31-45
Very low 136-180 37-48 46-60
Method of application:
a) Full rate of P and K and half of N should be applied as basal during final land
preparation.

b) The rest half of N should be top dressed after 25 days of sowing/planting.

c) After each cut (at 30 — 40 days intervals), half of the recommended rate of N should
be topdressed.
