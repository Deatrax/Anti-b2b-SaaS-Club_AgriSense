BARLEY (Hordeum vulgare)

(Var. BARI Barley-1 and BARI Barley-2)

Yield Goal: 3.0 + 0.3 t/ha

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha)

Interpretation N P K S Zn

Optimum 0-20 0-7 0-17 0-3 —

Medium 21-40 8-14 18-34 4-6 0.0-1.0

Low 41-60 15-21 35-51 7-9 1.1-2.0

Very low 61-80 22-28 52-68 10-12 2.1-3.0
Method of application:

a) Half of nitrogen and all of phosphorus, potassium, sulphur and zinc should be applied
as basal during final land preparation.

b) Remaining half nitrogen should be applied as topdress in two equal splits at 30-35
days after sowing (DAS) and 55—60 DAS after irrigation.

c) Under rainfed culture all fertilizers should be applied as broadcast during final land
preparation.
