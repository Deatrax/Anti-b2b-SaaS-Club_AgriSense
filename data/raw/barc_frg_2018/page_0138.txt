Method of application:

a) All of organic manure, phosphorus, potassium and sulphur; and one-third of nitrogen
should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Remaining nitrogen should be applied in two equal splits at 15 and 35 days after
sowing under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil as soon as
possible for better utilization.
