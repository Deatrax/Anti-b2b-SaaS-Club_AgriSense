Method of application:

a) All of organic manure, phosphorus, sulphur, magnesium, zinc and boron; and half of
nitrogen and potassium should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
Remaining half nitrogen and potassium should be applied as side dressing at 30-35

days after planting during earthing up operation.

b) Under rainfed condition all fertilizers should be applied during final land preparation.
