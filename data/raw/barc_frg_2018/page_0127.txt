Method of application:

a) Half of organic manure and all of phosphorus, sulphur, zinc and boron should be
applied as basal during final land preparation. Remaining organic manure should be
applied in pits before planting of seedlings.

b) Nitrogen and potassium should be applied in two equal splits at 15 and 35 days after
transplanting as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the
soil as soon as possible for better utilization.
