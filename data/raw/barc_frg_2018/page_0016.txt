Fig. 1 Relationship between soil pH and nutrient availability

Soil pH is the most important factor controlling nutrient availability in soils. Generally,
availability of macronutrients and Mo increases as soil pH increases and reverse is true for
micronutrients except Mo. Again, P availability is low in acid soils as well as in calcareous
soils. The effect of soil pH on nutrient availability is clearly illustrated in Fig. 1. In most
cases, pH 6-7 is optimum for adequate availability of nutrients in soils.
