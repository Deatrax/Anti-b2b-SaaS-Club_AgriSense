5.5 Deep Placement of Urea

Urea Super Granules (USG) and Urea Mega Granules (UMG) can be used to increase
nitrogen use efficiency (NUE). Urea Super Granules (USG) are small sized granules, each
weighing 0.9g and Urea Mega Granules (UMG) are large sized granules, each 1.8g or 2.7g
pellets made of ordinary prilled/granular urea by compressing. The amount of USG or
UMG should be adjusted to the recommended dose of N for different crops and soils. The
granules (USG/UMG) need to be placed after 7-10 days of transplanting of rice at 7-10 cm
soil depth at the centre of every four hills between rows | and 2, between rows 3 and 4, and so
on.
