GUINEA GRASS
(Panicum maximum)

Yield Goal (Green biomass): 120 + 12 t/ha/year

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha/year)
Interpretation N P K
Optimum 0-45 0-10 0-12
Medium 46-90 11-20 13-24
Low 91-135 21-30 25-36
Very low 136-180 31-40 37-48
Method of application:
a) Full rate of P and K and half of N should be applied as basal during final land
preparation.

b) The rest half of N should be top dressed after 25 days of sowing/planting.

c) After each cut (at 30 — 40 days intervals), half of the recommended rate of N should be
top dressed.
