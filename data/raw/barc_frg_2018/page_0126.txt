Method of application:

a) All of organic manure, phosphorus and sulphur; and half of nitrogen and potassium
should be applied as basal during final land preparation.

b) Remaining half nitrogen and potassium should be applied at 20-25 days after sowing
under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil as soon as possible for
better utilization.
