ORCHID (Phaius tankervilleae)

(Var. BARI Orchid-1)

Yield goal: About 18,000 flower sticks/ha

Soil Analysis Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) ‘inh °
Interpretation

N P K CD or PM*
Optimum 0-22 0-10 0-20
Medium 23-44 11-20 2140 5 7
Low 45-66 21-30 41-60
Very low 67-88 31-40 61-80

*CD: Cowdung PM: Poultry manure

Method of application:

a) All of cowdung, phosphorus and potassium should be applied as basal during final
land preparation.

b) Nitrogen should be applied as topdressing in three equal splits at 30, 60 and 90 days
after transplanting and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

ORCHID (Dendrobium spp.)

(Epiphytic orchid: Dendrobium)

Yield goal: 2-3 flower sticks/plant/year

Nutrient Recommendation (g/10 plant)
N P K
1.1 0.7 1.3
Method of application:

All of N, P and K should be dissolved in one litre of water and be sprayed on 10 plants.
Spraying should be done regularly at one month interval.
