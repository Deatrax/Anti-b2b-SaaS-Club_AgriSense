Method of application:

a) All of phosphorus and zinc; and half of nitrogen and potassium should be applied
during April-May with on set of shower. Remaining half nitrogen and potassium
should be applied within July to first week of August.

b) (NHz4)2SOz is generally used in tea cultivation as the source of nitrogen if it is not
mentioned otherwise. TSP and MoP might be used as the sources of P and K
respectively.

c) Application of zinc at the early stage encourages vegetative growth. Zinc fertilizer

should be applied in soil at the time of using other fertilizers.

d) If soil pH is below 5.0, dolomite [(CaMg(CO3),] application is suggested, usually

after a good shower.
